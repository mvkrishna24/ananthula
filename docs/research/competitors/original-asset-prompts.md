## Asset: Family Anchor 01 — **the priority asset**

### Reference lessons
- **Deliberate wardrobe non-coordination** (Chandana Brothers hero): one frame
  showing traditional, contemporary and children's dress simultaneously says
  "one house, every generation" with no copy. Best strategic idea in the study.
- **Reserve one side of frame for typography** (SISM couples banner, RSB men's
  banner, CSM brand-mood slide): all the strongest frames do this.
- **Warm directional side light with real cast shadow** (RSB saree hero) is what
  separates a photograph from a composite.
- **Group weighted to one side** rather than centred (SISM couples banner).

### Do NOT copy
- The Chandana Brothers family, their poses, their wardrobe combination, their
  hand-holding chain, their branded shopping bags, or their beige-gradient ground.
- Any competitor model, backdrop, prop arrangement or logo lockup.
- Cut-out-on-gradient treatment of any kind.

### Ananthula objective
Prove — not claim — that this is a family fashion house. Three generations in one
credible frame, in daylight, in a Warangal-appropriate setting, with the house's
own flagship category visible on more than one person. This is the chapter the
category leaders left unbuilt.

### Intended file
`public/images/family/family-celebration.webp` *(new manifest slot: `familyAnchor`)*

### Runway model
`nano-banana-pro`

### Ratio
16:9

### Resolution
2K

### Prompt
> Ultra-photorealistic editorial photograph of a multi-generational South Indian
> family at a festive gathering, for a heritage family-fashion campaign in
> Warangal, Telangana. Five people in a warm daylight courtyard: a grandmother in
> a muted emerald pattu silk saree with a fine antique-gold zari border; a mother
> in a soft turmeric-gold silk saree; a father in an ivory festive kurta; a girl
> of about seven in a sandalwood-and-rose pattu pavadai; a boy of about five in a
> small ivory kurta. The group sits right-of-centre in a loose triangle,
> mid-moment and genuinely interacting — the boy laughing while being lifted or
> tugging his father's hand, the grandmother turning toward him with real warmth,
> eyelines landing on each other rather than on the camera. Nobody poses for the
> lens. Wardrobe is deliberately varied rather than matching — traditional silk
> beside contemporary festive dress — so one frame reads as an entire household.
> The LEFT 30% is quiet warm plaster wall in soft shade, free of objects, for
> editorial typography. Late-morning warm daylight from the upper right, softer
> and brighter than an evening frame, with real directional cast shadows on the
> plaster and natural fabric movement. Environment: an unadorned South Indian
> courtyard veranda — warm aged plaster, dark timber posts, softly defocused
> depth. Colours: turmeric, sandalwood, ivory, muted emerald, antique gold, warm
> neutral shadow. 50mm editorial realism, moderate depth of field, fine film
> grain, realistic warm Indian skin texture, all hands anatomically correct.
> No bridal styling, no garlands, no mandap, no matching outfits, no
> corporate-stock grins, no palace props, no cut-outs, no flat gradient
> backgrounds, no text, no logo, no watermark.

### QA gate
- skin realism ≥ 9
- hand anatomy ≥ 9 (all ten hands)
- wardrobe realism ≥ 9
- **family relationship credibility ≥ 9** — eyelines must land on each other
- **three generations clearly present** — pass/fail
- **flagship silk visible on at least two people** — pass/fail
- campaign consistency with locked hero ≥ 9
- left-third typography zone clean — pass/fail

## Asset: Wedding Bride 01

### Reference lessons
- Reserve one side for type (SISM couples banner: right half empty with hairline rules).
- Fan the pallu into frame so the border is the product (SISM silk hero).
- Mobile crop should prioritise weave and jewellery legibility at thumb size
  (CSM bridal swing mobile variant).

### Do NOT copy
- CSM's flower swing, colonnade, lily pond or swans; SISM's painted mandapam canvas.
- Their jewellery intensity — in both references the temple set out-competes the saree.

### Ananthula objective
A bride who is quieter and more emotionally still than anything in the
competitive set, where the silk — not the jewellery — is the subject.

### Intended file
`public/images/wedding/wedding-bride.webp`

### Runway model / ratio / resolution
`nano-banana-pro` · 3:4 · 2K

### Prompt
Use prompt **02** in `docs/IMAGE_GENERATION_PROMPTS.md` unchanged — the research
validates it. One refinement to add: *"jewellery restrained so the silk remains
the subject; the pallu falls into frame showing the full border."*

### QA gate
skin ≥ 9 · hand anatomy ≥ 9 · silk realism ≥ 9 · **jewellery must not dominate the weave** · campaign consistency ≥ 9

## Asset: Wedding Groom 01

### Reference lessons
- Ivory/cream groom palette separates cleanly from bridal arakku (RSB men's hero).
- Restraint reads as premium — no turban, no garland (RSB).
- 40% of frame reserved for copy works at hero scale (RSB).

### Do NOT copy
- **The sherwani-and-Mughal-arch default.** Three of four competitors reach for
  North Indian visual language. This is the clearest differentiation available.
- RSB's ivory panelling, gold trellis, brass lanterns or floral urn.

### Ananthula objective
A recognisably **South Indian** groom: silk kurta with angavastram, in warm
Warangal-appropriate architecture. Only one frame in the entire competitive set
(SISM's Wedding Mahotsav groom) styles this way — and it is buried in a composite.

### Intended file
`public/images/wedding/wedding-groom.webp`

### Runway model / ratio / resolution
`nano-banana-pro` · 3:4 · 2K

### Prompt
Use prompt **03** in `docs/IMAGE_GENERATION_PROMPTS.md` — it already specifies
ivory raw-silk kurta with a gold-bordered angavastram, which the research
confirms as correct and differentiating. Add: *"no sherwani, no turban, no
garland, no Mughal arch."*

### QA gate
skin ≥ 9 · hand anatomy ≥ 9 · **South Indian styling — pass/fail** · same light and world as the bride frame ≥ 9
