## Evidence — South India Shopping Mall

- **Visual campaign quality 2.5** — two genuinely built sets (temple-courtyard silk, mandapam couple) share a carousel with 3-D gold "KILO SALE" lettering and a cut-out celebrity on a stock palace.
- **Pattu authority 3.0** — Silk 208 / Designer 791 / Handloom 28 plus a real Kanchipuram–Dharmavaram–Gadwal–Pochampally sourcing claim on About; but the 87-product Wedding Pattu collection is unlinked and there is no fabric facet.
- **Family positioning 2.0** — asserted by a celebrity line ("OUR FAVOURITE FAMILY SHOPPING MALL") and one testimonial; contradicted by a Women/Men-only nav and zero children in any campaign image.
- **Women 4.0 / Men 4.0** — 1,907 and 1,319 live products; men gets three homepage blocks.
- **Kids 0.5** — 19 products, no nav entry, no imagery, no homepage presence.
- **Wedding 3.0** — two wedding heroes and a named `Wedding Mahotsav` campaign, but no wedding landing page and no occasion nav.
- **Seasonal 3.0** — a live Aashadam/Kilo-Sale hero, undercut by an expired countdown and festival editorial that stops at Ugadi 2025.
- **Sale 2.5** — clear rupee-off badges and struck prices, but `Sale → /collections/all` (3,573 products) and 0 badges on the entire men's landing.
- **New arrivals 2.5** — a `FRESH ADDITIONS` rail exists, but the `new-release` collection holds all 3,573 products.
- **Store discovery 4.5** — 39 branches with addresses and Directions, an inline map finder plus a global drawer, tri-state footprint, new-store announcement slot.
- **Heritage 2.5** — three decades, named founders, Silk Mark/Handloom Mark, ISO 9001:2000 — all buried as prose on one page, never surfaced in the shopping flow.
- **Editorial 2.0** — twelve on-brief articles with a homepage rail, newest festival piece a year stale.
- **Mobile 3.5** — six true desktop/mobile hero pairs (2:1 vs 2:3), verified as re-layouts; both variants ship in the DOM and no other block gets mobile art.
- **Ecommerce 3.5** — 3,573 products, infinite scroll, sort/price/colour filters; only three facets on a 1,900-product landing and no shipping/returns reassurance.

## Reading the matrix

**No competitor scores above 3.0 on Kids, and none above 3.0 on Family
positioning.** `inference` — the "family fashion house" position is claimed by
everyone and owned by no one. It is available.

**Heritage storytelling is the weakest column overall** (mean 1.4). Two of four
have literally empty About pages. `inference` — Ananthula's 1951 story is not
just a differentiator, it is unopposed.

**Mobile campaign strategy is the strongest column** (mean 3.25). `source-derived`
— three of four author genuinely separate desktop and mobile hero art rather than
cropping. This is the one craft habit worth copying outright, and Ananthula
already does it via `mobilePosition` in the media manifest.
